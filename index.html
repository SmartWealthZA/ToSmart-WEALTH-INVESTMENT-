Investment provider options

Smart Wealth Investments is designed to present information from more than one suitably authorised investment provider. Provider names, products, prices, performance figures and account links will appear only after each provider has been independently verified and has approved the relevant relationship and data access.

▣

Provider verification

Confirm each provider's authorisation, product permissions, service terms and customer protections before listing it.

⇄

Separate provider accounts

Customer accounts and investments remain with the relevant provider. The profile must not imply that Smart Wealth Investments holds client money unless properly authorised to do so.

▥

Verified information

Fund details, valuations and performance are displayed only from approved provider sources, with dates, fees and risk disclosures.
